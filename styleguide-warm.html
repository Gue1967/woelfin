Copy Principles · Short. Precise. No superlatives.
· Serif italic for brand voice lines (tagline, ambassador)
· Sans for operational copy (scarcity, selection criteria)
· Never "luxury" or "premium" — show, don't tell
· Restraint over emphasis: no exclamation marks in brand copy
