Chapter I

Tennis

Saturday morning. Same court. Same hour.

2024 · Germany & Portugal
Tennis is one of the few sports
that never lost its culture.

The club. The court. The hour.
The ritual of preparation.
We started with tennis because tennis was ready for us. The sport already understood restraint. It already understood that the gesture matters more than the result. We built the first pieces for the players who understood this — and who had stopped finding clothes that matched what they knew.

WÖLFIN does not make sportswear. It makes clothes for people who take sport seriously enough to want something beyond performance markers and visible logos. The first chapter is not a collection. It is a position. Three pieces, two countries, one culture that has been consistent for over a hundred years and has no interest in changing what matters.
