Journal · Chapter I · Tennis

"These are not endorsements.
They are alignments."

WÖLFIN does not seek reach.
It seeks legitimacy.

Each ambassador was chosen because they represent the sport with honesty — not for the tournament, but for the practice. They wear pieces that are not yet available, in contexts that are not yet public.

This is the foundation being built.

Anna K.

Founding Ambassador TC Grün-Weiss Berlin

Anna has played at TC Grün-Weiss for eleven years.

She is the kind of player who arrives before the court opens.
Not for the tournament. For the practice.

She tested WÖLFIN's first prototype on a Tuesday morning in March. Her feedback shaped the final construction of the Performance Tee. She did not ask to post anything.

02

Marta V.

Founding Ambassador Clube de Ténis do Porto

Marta competed at national level for six years.
She now trains four times a week and coaches on weekends.

She understands the body in motion the way engineers understand tension — through repetition, not theory.

When asked what she noticed first about the Court Short, she said: "it moves with me, not after me." That sentence became the brief for the final cut.

03

Luís F.

Founding Ambassador MTTC Iphitos München

Luís moved from Lisbon to Munich in 2019.
He joined MTTC Iphitos and has played in every Bundesliga season since.

His relationship with the sport is one of discipline and longevity — the same court, different seasons.

He was the first person to wear the Club Jacket off-court. Nobody asked where it was from. Three people asked where they could find it.
