Forest & Linen — Fusion Direction

WÖLFIN
Style Guide

The definitive WÖLFIN palette fusion. Warm linen ground from Brunello and Loro Piana refinement, met by deep forest green — the wolf, the forest, the brand's own nature. Cream and hunter green is classic quiet luxury. This guide documents every component as it should appear in the fusion direction.

Direction Forest & Linen
Ground Brunello Cucinelli · Loro Piana · The Row
Accent Dark Forest Green — WÖLFIN Brand DNA
Typefaces Cormorant Garamond · Raleway
